Hi SJ,

Regarding the alternative superior feature to replace watermarks [1], I
am very interested in helping you design or/and implement it.

To share my context - I am primarily using DAMON_RECLAIM on my personal
laptop.  During my usage, I found that using FREE% as the default
watermark metric is highly problematic for modern Linux desktop/laptop
environments, because the kernel aggressively uses free memory for page
cache.  Therefore, I chose to compile a kernel myself first, which
supports monitoring AVAILABLE%, because I understood that the current
watermark mechanism is not planned for long-term extension, I chose not
to propose further improvements to it, and instead focused on
understanding why that direction is limited.

But I am very interested in understanding the design constraints behind
deprecating watermarks.  From my current perspective, some enhancements
such as additional watermark metrics and always-on monitoring, seem
relatively straightforward to implement.  However, I assume there is a
deeper reason why you consider this direction unsuitable for long-term
evolution.  If you could share a bit about those constraints, I would be
able to think about the new mechanism in the right direction, rather
than approaching it from my own assumptions.

However, my need for more accurate trigger metric (like AVAILABLE% for
desktop scenarios) and always monitoring is exactly why I want to help
you build the new mechanism. And I do not want to add anything to a
mechanism that is about to be deprecated.

If you are open to this, I would be happy to start by reviewing the
design direction you have in mind, and then help with implementation
where it is feasible for me to contribute meaningfully.
